Solana Prediction Market Competitive Landscape

Generated on 2026-04-22 for a fresh prediction market idea on Solana.

Crowdedness: Crowded Moat: Distribution + liquidity + trust Best opening: verticalized or private markets

Direct Competitors

  • Drift BET: native Solana prediction market embedded in Drift's trading stack.
  • MetaDAO: futarchy markets for governance and launch decisions, not general retail prediction.
  • Hedgehog: long-running Solana prediction-market brand with broad-market positioning.
  • TBD: new Solana protocol focused on verified human opinion using World ID.
  • Kalshi via Phantom/DFlow: not natively on-chain matching, but distributed to Solana wallets as SPL positions.

Substitutes

  • Polymarket: category leader, strong mindshare, deep liquidity, not on Solana.
  • Kalshi / Robinhood: regulated event contracts for U.S. users.
  • SX Bet / other betting apps: adjacent outcome markets for sports and events.
  • Off-chain polling and research tools: surveys, Discord voting, governance forums.

Dead or Weak Signals

  • Many public Solana prediction-market repos exist, but most appear to be demos, hackathon projects, or low-traction forks.
  • Older generalized consumer prediction markets on Solana did not become clear category winners.
  • This suggests distribution and liquidity, not contract design alone, are the hard parts.

Takeaways

  • The space is not empty. Multiple teams have already built on Solana, and at least four live approaches exist today.
  • The real wedge is probably not "a generic Polymarket on Solana". That lane is already contested.
  • The strongest defensible angles look like private markets, creator-led markets, governance/futarchy, or opinion markets with identity/reputation data.
  • The primary moat is not smart contracts. It is distribution, trustworthy resolution, and sustained liquidity.
